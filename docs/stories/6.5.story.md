## Dev Notes

### Why
The homepage is the highest-traffic page and showcases **all four net-new Atlas components** that have no current React equivalent (animated hero, marquee, interactive ink results chart, split service panels). Stories 6.1–6.4 laid the foundation: 6.1 tokens/fonts/`.wrap`/shadows, 6.2 dark-mode removal, 6.3 header/footer/`ScrollProgress`/`LogoMark`, 6.4 re-skinned `Button`/fields/`Badge`/`Card`/`Accordion`. This story consumes all of that and is the first **page** re-skin — it also creates the **shared reveal/count-up hooks** that the later page stories (6.6–6.11) and 6.12 reuse. The work is mostly net-new component construction + porting two vanilla-JS animation files (`atlas.js` counters/reveal, `home.js` hero entrance + interactive chart) to idiomatic React.
