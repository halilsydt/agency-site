## Dev Notes

### Why
The Pricing page is the conversion-critical page linked from the homepage `pricing-preview` panels (6.5) and the service-page CTAs (6.6). Its Atlas structure is: centered `.phero.cc` hero → centered `.seg` platform toggle → 3-column `.tiers` grid of `.tier` cards (with the popular tier's green `.pop-badge` treatment) → green `.bundle` bar → [FAQ — deferred to 6.10] → `.cta` close. This is mostly a markup/skin swap on four reused components (`pricing-hero`, `pricing-section`, `pricing-card`, `bundle-highlight`) plus the page client. There are **no net-new bespoke JS components** here (unlike 6.5's chart/marquee) — the only interactivity is the existing platform toggle (already wired via `useState` + `PillToggle`) and reveal-on-scroll (shared 6.5 `<Reveal>`). The main subtleties are: the `.pop-badge`/`.tier.popular` green treatment (replacing the old shadcn `ring-green`), the `.seg` toggle gaining platform icons while keeping its accessible button name, the `860px` (not `820px`) tier collapse, and the hero-headline `.mark` i18n (like 6.6).
