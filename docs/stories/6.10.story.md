## Story

**As a** developer re-skinning the Scalenty marketing site to the Atlas design system,
**I want** the FAQ page (`/faq`) re-skinned to Atlas — the centered `.phero.cc` interior hero with a green `.mark` on "questions", the Atlas `.faq-cats` filter row (pill `.faq-cat` buttons with an **ink active state**), the `.faq-list` of `details.q` accordion cards (already styled to Atlas in 6.4), and the closing centered `.cta` block (green "Contact Us" + ghost "View Pricing") — replacing the generic centered `bg-muted/30` hero + `Container`-wrapped `Button`-pill filter + plain CTA markup,
**so that** `/faq` matches the Atlas reference (`docs/design system/atlas/FAQ.html`) while **preserving the category-filter behavior, the accordion expand/collapse + keyboard/ARIA accessibility, i18n (EN/TR), routing, SEO metadata, and analytics** — building on the Atlas tokens (6.1), dark-mode removal (6.2), header/footer chrome (6.3), the **6.4-re-skinned `Accordion` (`details.q` look, Radix kept)** and `Button` primitives, the shared `Reveal`/`useReveal`/`useReducedMotion` utilities + the homepage closing-`.cta` pattern (6.5), and the `heroEyebrow` + `heroTitle` `{ pre, mark, post }` i18n triple + centered `.phero.cc` hero precedent established by the service pages (6.6), pricing page (6.7), about page (6.8), and contact page (6.9).
