## Dev Notes

### Why
The Amazon + Etsy service pages are the platform-specific "deep-dive" pages linked from the homepage `services-overview` panels (6.5). They share a near-identical structure (`phero` hero → `svc-detail` grid → `.cta`) differing only in **platform accent** (Amazon green, Etsy clay) and copy. This story is mostly a markup/skin swap on three reused components (`service-hero`, `service-list`, `service-card`) — there are **no net-new bespoke JS components** here (unlike 6.5's chart/marquee). It reuses the shared `Reveal`/`useReveal` utilities created in 6.5 for the reveal-on-scroll, and the `Button` `btn-green`/`btn-ghost` variants from 6.4. The main subtleties are: the platform-accent split, the `.sd.wide` full-width final Amazon card, the Etsy icon-map gap (`search`/`megaphone`), and the hero-headline i18n re-mapping (the Atlas h1 is a different sentence than today's page title).
