## Story

**As a** developer re-skinning the Scalenty marketing site to the Atlas design system,
**I want** the About page (`/about`) re-skinned to Atlas — the `.phero` interior hero with a green `.mark`, the two-column `.about-grid` Mission row (text + emerald `.viz-card` metric card), the alternating `.about-grid` Approach row (ink-gradient `.ph-img` photo placeholder + `.about-points` check list with the clay eyebrow), the ink `.show` stats band (`.stats-band` of four `.stat`s), the centered `.sec-head.cc` Team header + `.team-grid` of `.member` cards (gradient `.avatar`), and the closing `.cta` — replacing the generic `AboutHero` + `MissionSection` + `ApproachSection` + `ExperienceHighlights` + `TeamSection`/`TeamCard` markup,
**so that** `/about` matches the Atlas reference (`docs/design system/atlas/About.html`) while preserving i18n (EN/TR), routing, all content (mission/approach/highlights/team), the bottom-CTA links, SEO metadata, and analytics — building on the Atlas tokens (6.1), dark-mode removal (6.2), header/footer chrome (6.3), re-skinned UI primitives incl. `Button`/`Card` (6.4), the shared `Reveal`/`useReveal`/`useReducedMotion` utilities + the homepage `hero` `.viz-card` and `results-gallery` `.show` ink-band patterns (6.5), and the `heroTitle` `{ pre, mark, post }` i18n triple + inline-`.cta` precedent established by the service pages (6.6) and pricing page (6.7).
