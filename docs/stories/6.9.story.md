## Dev Notes

### Why
The Contact page is the primary conversion surface — it must keep the **Formspree form** and **Cal.com booking** working while adopting the Atlas skin. Its Atlas structure is simple and **two-column**: a left-aligned `.phero` hero (green `.mark` on "growth") → a `.contact-grid` (`1.1fr .9fr`) with a **`.form-card`** surface card (the message form) on the left and a **stacked right column** = ink **`.call-card`** ("book a call" → Cal.com) + a **`.contact-info`** list of four labeled `.ci` rows (email/phone/remote/response). Unlike 6.5/6.8 there are **no net-new bespoke JS components** and the form internals already wear the Atlas `.field` look from 6.4 — so the work is a **markup/skin swap** across `contact-hero` + `contact-info` + the page-client layout, plus wrapping the existing `ContactForm` in the `.form-card` and replacing the inline calendar column with the `BookingDialog` trigger. The main subtleties are: **preserving the form + booking behavior** (skin only, no logic change), the **layout reshape** (the old centered-info-below-grid becomes a stacked right column), the **hero `.mark` i18n triple**, re-skinning the form's legacy `green-50`/`red-50` panels to Atlas tokens, and the `.contact-info` test changes (drop the "Or reach us directly" heading; richer `.ci` rows).
